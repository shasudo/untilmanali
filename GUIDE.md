# UntilManali — how to use it

For the 6: Shathwik, Koushik, Deepika, Srinivas, Rushi (CR), Sonika.

## Open it

1. Go to the app address in your phone browser.
2. Add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone,
   menu → Add to Home screen on Android). It then works like an app,
   even with patchy network.
3. No login, no code — it opens straight into the crew. First thing: tap your face.

## Tell it who you are

First launch asks once: tap YOUR face, and the phone remembers it after that.
It turns lime with a star. Everything personal — "you owe / you get back" —
follows that chip.

## Stick a new bill

1. Tap **+ stick a new bill**.
2. Name it loosely: "fuel after chandigarh", "maggi x6". Amount in ₹.
3. The bill is always paid by YOU (your selected face) — no picker. Recording one someone else paid? Tap their face first, then stick it.
4. **Who was in on it** — tap faces to include or drop people.
   Only 3 of you went? Drop the other 3, the math follows.
5. Split it three ways:
   - **even steven** — split equally between who's in.
   - **exact ₹** — type each person's share. The line under the
     boxes tells you if your bits don't add up to the bill.
   - **by %** — shares must add to 100%.
6. **stick it up.** The ticket lands on the fridge instantly for all 6.

## Read the fridge

- The fridge opens on **your** bills only — pick your face on the wheel and that's your world.
- **show everyone** flips to the full crew view when you're curious.
- Owe someone? Only YOUR lines show **pay** — it opens GPay/PhonePe with their UPI ID and the exact amount filled in. When you return, the app asks: mark sorted, or not yet.
- Each ticket shows who paid, who owes what, and the total stub.
- Your own share is highlighted lime.
- Wrong ticket? **tear off** asks first. If payments point at it, choose: bill only, or bill + those payments ("gone for everyone, no undo").

## Settle up

Under your faces, lines like "Koushik owes Rushi ₹300" appear.
Pay the person (cash, UPI, whatever), then tap **sorted** — it asks you to confirm the exact from → to → amount first, so pocket-taps can't move money. Tap **yes, paid** only if money really moved; **cancel** records nothing.
When every share of a bill is covered, the bill tears itself off. Nothing to clean up.
Settle fast — that's the whole rule.

## What’s new

The **what’s new** jump on home is the changelog — everything the app learned to do, newest first. Only the dev can post lines (dev key), and the whole crew gets pinged about each one.

## Pings (notifications)

1. On launch a card asks to switch pings on — tap **switch on**, then **allow**.
2. Your phone now buzzes when anyone else sticks a bill, sorts one, or pins a go-to — even with the app closed.
3. Switching faces re-points pings automatically. Tap pings again to switch off.
4. iPhone needs the installed home-screen app (iOS 16.4+). Android just works.
5. If it says the ping service is unreachable, check internet, pause any adblocker, and tap again — desktop browsers are the fussy ones, phones almost always go through first try.
6. Every evening there's one automatic nudge if anything is still unsettled — it says so right in the message, so nobody thinks a friend is nagging.
 Florists of the trip, running jokes, "rushi paid, miracles happen".
Tap × on a note to unpin it. The wall is forever, the bills are not.

## The name

UntilManali is just the crew's name — there is no trip countdown, no date to set. Open the app, tap your face, spend.

## House rules

- Six people, no outsiders — don't pass the address around.
- Tear off and re-stick beats living with a wrong bill.
- Small bills count too — chai adds up.
- If the app looks stale, pull to refresh. If it looks broken,
  hard-refresh once (hold the refresh button → hard reload).
